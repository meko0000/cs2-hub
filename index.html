Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about CS2 sensitivity, viewmodels, and installing your autoexec.cfg.

eDPI (Effective Dots Per Inch) is calculated by multiplying your Mouse DPI by your in-game CS2 sensitivity (e.g., 800 DPI × 1.0 sensitivity = 800 eDPI). Most Tier-1 CS2 pros play between 600 and 1,100 eDPI, with the pro average sitting right around 800–880 eDPI. This range balances fast 180° turns with pixel-accurate headshot micro-adjustments.
Multiply your Valorant sensitivity by 3.1818 to get the exact same cm/360° distance in Counter-Strike 2. To convert from CS2 to Valorant, divide your CS2 sensitivity by 3.1818. Our converter above handles this automatically alongside Fortnite, Apex Legends, and Overwatch 2.
Place the downloaded autoexec.cfg into your CS2 cfg folder:
C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Counter-Strike Global Offensive\game\csgo\cfg\

Then right-click Counter-Strike 2 in Steam → Properties → Launch Options and add +exec autoexec.cfg so it loads automatically every time you launch the game.

In CS2, if viewmodel_presetpos is set to 1, 2, or 3 after your offset commands, it overrides your custom X, Y, and Z offsets. Always run viewmodel_presetpos 0 (or put it first in your autoexec) before applying custom offsets.
